# Local systems and technology source pack
**Mettelo BA-042 · Stage 3 · Oliver King's note, 20 July**

## Current landscape
e-RS supports the national referral service. PAS holds local appointments. A manually maintained tracker records local administrative work and shared notifications support communication. Neither the national platform's public feature list nor the analysis extract proves Westmere has a complete local interface.

PAS provides an overnight appointment extract. The tracker is updated by colleagues and may lag. No committed live two-way portal write-back is provided. A reconciled referral ID in the training data is not evidence that every production source already uses it consistently.

## Existing capability claims
IT believes local worklists, ownership fields and templates could be improved under current licences. Estimated internal effort is 12 analyst days at £450/day plus 8 interface-specialist days at £650/day, with £5,000–£8,000 annual support. No resource booking, audit coverage, peak performance or recovery demonstration is approved. Current tools are not a zero-cost option.

Message events, clinical review and appointment state have different owners. A technical import may succeed while some business records remain unmatched. No supplied evidence shows reconciliation after a missed file, out-of-order update or repeated resubmission.

## Supplier inputs, not a completed options appraisal
PatientRoute quote: £70,000 setup plus £18,000/year for the local portal; minimum two-year term. Excludes PAS connector (indicative £22,000), accessible letters/telephone support, clinical-safety work, IG review, data cleansing, training/backfill and VAT. Claims 30% fewer booking calls without a comparable study. The demo uses seeded data.

ServiceSuite quote: from £98,000 setup plus £28,000/year, three-year term. Claims combined referral/booking functionality but no binding Westmere interface or migration scope. Annual cost exceeds the initial ceiling; the BA must still explain relevant trade-offs rather than choose on price alone.

The existing-platform option might reduce duplicate maintenance, but suitability and internal capacity remain unproved. Process, governance, communication or sequenced changes are also legitimate alternatives for the participant to assess.

## Integration and access concerns
IT asks for the operational consequences of stale data, not a generic real-time slogan. A cancellation not reaching a view and an older aggregate count can have different consequences. Role restrictions, support arrangements, message ordering, repeated events and recovery need business definitions and acceptance evidence.

No production credentials or real records are provided. The August prototype is isolated; it is not a contracted PAS/e-RS capability. Supplier defaults and integration claims must be tested against the participant's own needs and clinical/governance constraints.

## Unestablished facts
Complete interface mapping, latency/availability/recovery criteria, role entitlements, final licence coverage, retention schedule, peak workload, migration rules and total cost are absent. The BA should make those dependencies visible and collaborate with owners rather than invent technical commitments or deliver a pre-completed architecture.
