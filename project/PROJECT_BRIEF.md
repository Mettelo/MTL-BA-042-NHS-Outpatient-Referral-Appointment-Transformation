# MTL-BA-042 — commissioned assignment

**Mettelo Business Analyst Project Studio · distinct pathway edition**

## Business problem and reason for commissioning the team

Routine dermatology and orthopaedic referrals pass through administrative checks, clinical triage and booking, but patients and GP practices cannot always tell who owns the next step. Repeated information requests, inconsistent status messages and capacity constraints may look like one queue problem even when they have different causes.

Westmere needs to decide what problem is worth solving before commissioning a portal or specifying a solution. Discovery must distinguish patient communication, administrative information quality, clinical hand-off and clinic capacity, and identify where existing evidence is insufficient.

Read the [organisation and sponsor context](../business_context/BUSINESS_CONTEXT_AND_SPONSOR_BRIEF.md) for the observed operating environment.

## Role and business decision
You are the Discovery and Service Business Analyst. What service problem should Westmere investigate or solve next, for whom, and what evidence justifies proceeding beyond discovery?

A discovery recommendation: proceed, investigate further, pivot or stop. No FRD, implementation plan, product backlog or UAT portfolio is required.

## Business situation and intended value

A GP sends a routine referral with attachments. An administrator sees a blank cover field and may return it even if related information is in an attachment. The GP and patient receive incomplete explanations of what happens next. A clinician still needs to make any clinical triage decision, and a usable appointment also depends on site, capacity, interpreter and communication needs. The service journey must be investigated rather than assumed.

The sponsor receives a defensible account of user needs, current service pain, evidence strength and remaining unknowns. Proposed experiments have learning questions and success/stop criteria. Discovery does not prove an improved waiting-time outcome or clinical safety.

## Scope

Routine adult dermatology and orthopaedic referral receipt, administrative validation, clinical-review hand-off, first appointment offer and associated communications. Study patient-facing and backstage service dependencies, accessibility and uncertainty.

No full FRD, use-case specification set, implementation business case, mandatory future-state process, product backlog or UAT portfolio is required. Clinical diagnosis, treatment, prioritisation and safety approval remain with authorised clinicians. Emergency/cancer pathways and replacing national e-RS are excluded.

## Working agreement
Use supplied facts, conflicting stakeholder accounts and synthetic source records. Make calculations reproducible; distinguish evidence, interpretation, proposal and unresolved approval. Reference actual source paths and row/case IDs. Do not invent stakeholder sign-off, real research, executed tests or realised benefits. External references supply context, not automatic approval of your proposals.
