# MTL-BA-042 — 4-week pathway

Weeks are relative to cohort start. Original source dates remain part of the fictional history; the facilitator may compress that history into release weeks.

| Week | Work and checkpoint |
|---|---|
| 1 | Frame discovery and research access. Review relevant task criteria and retain a dated revision. |
| 2 | Synthesise testimony and map the current service. Review relevant task criteria and retain a dated revision. |
| 3 | Quantify problems and challenge assumptions. Review relevant task criteria and retain a dated revision. |
| 4 | Prioritise needs and make the discovery decision. Review relevant task criteria and retain a dated revision. |

S1 begins in week 1; S2–S3 in week 2; S4–S5 in week 3; S6 in week 4.

The [manifest](RELEASE_MANIFEST.csv) controls when sources become available. Optional later experiment material has no mandatory UAT task. No elapsed simulation week constitutes stakeholder approval or an executed implementation.
