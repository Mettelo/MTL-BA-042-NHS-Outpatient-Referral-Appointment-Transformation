# Existing referral and booking forms/messages
**Mettelo BA-042 · Stage 3 · Fictional operational source copies**

## Referral cover sheet — March revision
Local referral reference: ____  
Referring practice / patient local reference: ____  
Requested specialty / referrer priority: ____  
Clinical attachment included: yes / no  
Cover information complete: yes / no  
Communication/interpreter/support need: ____  
Preferred contact: ____  
Admin stamp: received ____ / complete ____ / returned ____  

The form does not identify who reviewed the attachment, which version was considered, what ownership remains after a return or how the referrer acknowledged it. A priority recorded by a referrer is not permission for an administrator or algorithm to make a clinical decision.

## Local return-request macro
“Referral incomplete. Please resend the missing information. Your referral has been returned.”

The template has no controlled distinction between absent attachment, failed preview and clinically insufficient information. It does not identify who owns follow-up, whether a clinician saw the material or how the referrer should acknowledge the request. It is existing wording, not an approved future process.

Text sent: yes / no

Sending is not delivery or understanding. A tick for support required is not confirmation that support was arranged. The source does not provide a complete accessible-communications specification or a consent model.

## Cancellation and replacement messages
SMS: “Your outpatient appointment is cancelled. We will contact you.”
Letter: “An appointment has been arranged at [site] on [date]. Please contact us if you cannot attend.”

Neither supplied template includes a consistent earlier appointment reference or explanation of the relationship between cancellation and replacement. The coordinator has not supplied delivery ordering evidence. Do not claim this precise message caused every did_not_attend record.

## Current counter guidance
The local admin desk card says “return any incomplete mandatory field”. A consultant memo says attached content should receive appropriate review before a clinical-information return. The card has no clinical escalation or ownership detail. Their authority and interaction are disputed in the policy pack.

Use these source forms to investigate information, hand-offs, exceptions and communication. Participants must create their own field/rule proposals, future processes, acceptance criteria and validation record. No redesigned form or completed BA answer is supplied.
