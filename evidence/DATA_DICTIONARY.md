# Extraction notes and dictionary

Snapshot: 30 June 2026, 18:00 UK. Ten purposively selected referrals received in June; one row per referral in turnaround data. IDs PTxxx are fictional local identifiers, not NHS numbers. Blank dates mean not observed by cutoff. Dates are calendar dates; no clock-start or clock-stop interpretation for RTT is supplied.
Rejection log is event-level; F002 has two administrative returns before resubmission. Count unique referrals separately from events. 'attachment_present' says a file existed, not that it was usable or clinically sufficient.
Cancellation file has one row per appointment's latest recorded event at cutoff; future appointment dates are possible. AP01/AP02 are linked replacement appointments. DNA means did not attend and is distinct from cancellation. Clinic cancellation counts cannot be added to appointment cancellations without reconciliation.
First offer is the date an offer was recorded, not the appointment date, acceptance, treatment or RTT completion. Do not drop unresolved referrals when discussing waiting workload.
