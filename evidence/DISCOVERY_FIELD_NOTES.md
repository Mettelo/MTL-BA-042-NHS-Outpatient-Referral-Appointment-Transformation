# Mettelo BA-042 — pathway-specific source evidence

**Release 2 · fictional source material, not an approved solution**

## Discovery-only research supplement
All people and observations below are fictional. These are raw field notes from a small convenience sample, not findings to copy or a representative patient study.

8 July, telephone observation: a caller asks whether a returned referral means the specialist has decided they do not need care. The administrator can see a missing cover field but cannot see whether a clinician has reviewed the attachment. The caller describes waiting for a letter that the GP believes the hospital will send. No clinical conclusion is recorded.

9 July, GP practice desk: an administrator reports receiving two requests for the same attachment through different channels. They cannot establish whether both concern the same referral without checking local correspondence. The sample contains only one practice and excludes practices using other software.

10 July, patient representative discussion: Eleanor says online status would help some patients but her neighbour relies on a telephone contact and an interpreter. That neighbour was not interviewed. Do not claim their needs were validated.

13 July, booking observation: staff describe a clinic slot as available even while accessibility arrangements are unconfirmed. The service manager counts offered appointments; the booking colleague wants to understand whether a patient can use the offer. Neither definition alone establishes patient experience.

## Research access constraints
Only the supplied testimony may be treated as completed research. Propose additional interviews or usability sessions; do not contact patients, practices or NHS staff. An authorised clinician must assess safety. There is no complete non-attender study, patient demographic sample or complete external GP-system audit.

## Discovery review questions
The sponsor asks whether a digital status feature addresses the largest problem or merely the most visible complaint. Clinical staff ask whether a cover-field completeness rule would incorrectly return a referral with clinically adequate attachments. Booking asks whether additional demand visibility helps when clinic capacity changes. These questions remain open for participant synthesis.
