# Existing referral, booking and information-handling sources
**Mettelo BA-042 · Stage 3 · Collated 20 July**
All local instructions are fictional. Official NHS material is separately referenced; the pack does not invent national law or clinical criteria.

## Authority and conflicting sources
Outpatients owns local administrative procedure. Authorised clinicians own clinical interpretation, urgency and safety decisions. IG reviews information handling; PAS ownership does not itself give authority to alter a clinical pathway. No signed document in the source set defines the entire end-to-end journey.

Local procedure v1.4 says administrators check identity/reference, presence of attachments and mandatory cover fields; incomplete items are returned to the practice. A 12 May consultant memo says attached clinical information should receive appropriate review before a clinical-content return. Neither source defines the full rota, acknowledgement or residual ownership if the practice does not respond. The BA must investigate rather than declare one document an approved future rule.

## Clinical escalation and boundaries
Administrators must not diagnose, substitute a guessed urgency or discard a concern because a cover field is blank. Unclear or concerning content goes to the appropriate authorised clinical reviewer under the local escalation route. The supplied route is incomplete; the participant should identify decisions and missing evidence without designing clinical treatment thresholds.

Routine scope does not mean every incoming document can be handled without clinical oversight. Clinical-safety review and the relevant organisational approvals are required before implementing an automated clinical-content decision. No source provides approval or a full safety case.

## Booking and communication
A suitable appointment depends on specialty, site, clinic purpose and relevant support, not only a free calendar slot. Local guidance asks staff to record patient communication needs and preferences and to use an appropriate supported route. A mobile number alone is not proof of agreed digital-only contact.

A cancelled appointment and its replacement should be distinguishable in the retained source. The existing worksheet does not demonstrate reliable message order, delivery or understanding. No future communication SLA or accessibility acceptance criterion is prewritten here.

## Waiting-time measures
The local proposed five-working-day target starts at administrative completion and ends at recorded clinical triage. It is not a national RTT treatment measure. Return, cancellation, first offer and attendance do not automatically establish an RTT clock stop. No participant is authorised to change pathway clocks to improve a dashboard.

Use official definitions and the correct population before drawing any external comparison. The fictional records contain no real provider code and cannot be joined to national data as though they were a real trust submission.

## Access, purpose and retention
Use synthetic information for demonstration. Access to clinical attachments must have an appropriate purpose and role. Shared inbox membership is not blanket permission to export the contents to a supplier. Relevant patient communication/support information and full clinical content have different access considerations.

Retain appropriate decision and access evidence under the approved schedule; that schedule is not supplied. Do not invent a duration or assume retain everything. Supplier handling, access, incident and deletion arrangements remain subject to IG/procurement review.

## Current uncertainty
No approved future state, combined decision table, full ownership model or universal rejection rule is provided. Nadia's funding sponsorship does not waive clinical or governance decisions. These sources support the participant's own requirements/rules, conflict management, traceability and testing; they are not the completed outputs.
