# Scope and constraints

Routine adult referral receipt, administrative validation, clinician triage, booking and appointment changes. Include accessibility, patient choice, clinical escalation and information governance. Exclude emergency care, clinical diagnosis, treatment decisions and national e-RS redesign.

Local patient administration system (PAS) replacement is out of scope. Clinical priority decisions remain with authorised clinicians. No assumption of extra clinical sessions is approved. Use synthetic IDs rather than NHS numbers.

No production access is provided. Staff can attend one 45-minute workshop each week. Finance needs one-off and recurring costs separately, with assumptions about effort and adoption. Any change to policy requires the named policy owner; funding approval alone does not approve a control exception.
