# Change notice 01 — A more demanding local target without extra sessions
**Mettelo BA-042 · Stage 5A · Raised 3 August 2026 by Nadia Price**
Release after a saved baseline and the previous notice response. This is source evidence, not a completed impact assessment.

## Trigger and new information
The board proposes changing the local administrative-completion-to-triage ambition from 90% within five working days to 90% within three. No additional consultant sessions or staff are funded. The £120,000 one-off envelope includes £12,000 committed; the annual ceiling remains £20,000.

The engagement still requires a defensible business decision. A proposed target, supplier default or rota constraint does not itself approve a future process, clinical rule, purchase or go-live. Preserve the earlier source and your interpretation before recording the new information.

## Supporting attachment
The board minute explicitly calls this a proposed local operational target. It is not a change to national RTT standards. F0701 remains an example where administrative completion itself is unresolved; improving a completed-only measure may leave that work outside the denominator.

No complete safety case, new capacity study, representative patient research or signed future requirements is attached. Record which evidence is sufficient for a decision and which remains missing.

The initial funding and desired 12 October operational pilot remain unless this notice explicitly changes them. Policy, clinical and governance obligations are not waived. No production data or live implementation is authorised.
